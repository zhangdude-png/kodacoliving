# KodaLiving (Koda Coliving.AI) — build spec

Brand: "Koda Coliving.AI" wordmark with "AI" badge, subline "AUTONOMOUS OPS". Design system "Elevate Coliving": primary #60308D (purple), secondary #44898C (teal), tertiary #5E4400, neutral #F5F5F5; headings Montserrat. Purple gradient headlines on landing pages, light dashboards.

Product: agentic AI coliving-management SaaS for coliving operators and landlords in Singapore. AI agents handle WhatsApp/WeChat customer service and leads, viewings (AI Property Agent), tenancy agreements and onboarding, rent invoicing/collection, vendor/maintenance dispatch.
